Real vanilla, from people we know.

We sell Ndali vanilla — pods, extract and powder — grown on a family estate in Uganda and sold direct in the UK. No brokers, no re-bottling, and nothing synthetic anywhere near it.

Vanilla is a climbing orchid. Every flower is pollinated by hand, and every pod takes about nine months to grow and cure. That is why real vanilla costs what it costs, and why most of what is sold as “vanilla” in a supermarket has never been near the plant.
