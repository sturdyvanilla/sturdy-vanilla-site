Privacy policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Sturdy Vanilla is a small direct-to-consumer business in Somerset, United Kingdom, selling Ndali vanilla products. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and what you can do about it. We are the data controller for the purposes of UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

The short version

We hold as little as we can. We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and our own software reads only our own business accounts, never anyone else's.

Our use of the TikTok API

We operate private, internal software that connects to TikTok's APIs to manage the @sturdyvanilla account. It is not a public product, it has no users other than us, and it is not available for anyone else to sign in to.

  • It authenticates only against our own TikTok account, using our own authorisation.
  • It reads our own profile information, our own video list, and the performance statistics for videos we ourselves have published.
  • It uploads video content we have created to our own account, for us to review and publish.
  • It does not access, collect, scrape or store data belonging to any other TikTok user, creator or account.
  • It does not collect information about people who view or engage with our content beyond the aggregate, anonymous statistics TikTok itself provides (such as total views and average watch time). These contain no personal data and cannot be traced back to an individual.

Access tokens for this software are stored privately and are never shared with any third party. Data retrieved from TikTok is stored only for as long as it is useful for reviewing our own content performance, and is deleted when it is not.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers we need to run the business, and only what they need:

  • TikTok Shop: the sales platform, which processes your order and payment.
  • Delivery and fulfilment partners: to get your parcel to you.
  • Email and cloud hosting providers: to receive your messages and run our systems.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not pass it to advertisers or data brokers. Some providers operate outside the UK; where they do, transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep things

Order and transaction records are kept for six years, as UK tax law requires. Correspondence is kept for up to two years. Content performance data is kept for as long as it is useful and then deleted.

This website

This site is a static set of pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and does not track you. The typeface and the small amount of script it uses are both served from this site, never from a third party. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which we do not access for marketing.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have four rights. Ask us for a copy of your data. Ask us to correct or delete it. Object to, or restrict, how we use it. Ask for it in a portable format. Email joe@sturdy.vip and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
